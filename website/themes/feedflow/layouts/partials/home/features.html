Features

Freshness, not obligation.

A reader designed around attention instead of unread counts.

Set your pace

Choose a Flash, Daily, Standard, Slow, or Timeless window. Recent stories stay clear while older ones gently fade and leave the Flow. There is no unread number waiting to be cleared.

Save or let go

Keep an article in Saved when it deserves lasting attention. Use Let Go when it does not. It disappears locally and can be undone, without pretending you read it or changing its remote read state.

Your voices, your streams

Follow independent voices, collect them into streams, and keep your reading chronological and algorithm-free. Reader Mode, offline content, search, OPML, and optional Android sync remain available when you need them.
